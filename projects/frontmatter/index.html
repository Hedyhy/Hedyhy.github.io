FrontMatter 观铺

Find the opportunity. Measure the risk.

An AI-powered storefront feasibility platform that helps small merchants in Singapore evaluate space, rent, risk and business potential before signing a lease. Users upload a shop photo and lease details; FrontMatter returns a traceable 0–100 lease score, risk flags, a cash-flow model and what-if controls.

The problem

Before the first customer walks in, the lease has already started counting. Small founders rely on brokers, listing photos, Google Maps and rough spreadsheets that never add up to one decision.

307F&B closures per month in Singapore in 2025, up from 254 in 2024
60%of outlets closed Jan–Oct 2025 had operated for less than five years
82%of those under-five-year closures had never made a profit

How it works

One workflow connects the storefront image, lease terms, location context and financial risk.

  1. Lease inputsRent, area, lease term, fit-out budget and business type.
  2. Spatial AIReads frontage, layout, visibility and utilities from storefront or floorplan photos.
  3. Local groundingNearby merchants, competition and Singapore commercial context via Google Places.
  4. F&B rule checkWater, power, gas, floor trap, grease trap, exhaust, wastewater and approved use.
  5. Economics modelNPV, IRR, discounted payback, break-even customers and BASE / DOWN / SEVERE scenarios.
  6. Decision reportScore breakdown, risk flags, verdict and live what-if controls for traffic, ticket size and rent.

The product

The live prototype: lease intake on the left, the decision workspace on the right.

Not a chatbot: a quantified lease evaluation engine

Hybrid scoring: 60% deterministic rules, 40% AI judgement, so every score is traceable.

Vision layer

Turns storefront and interior photos into a structured spatial blueprint, flagging missing evidence for F&B setup.

Grounding layer

Anchors the site in Singapore’s real commercial and competitive context using map data and official datasets.

Rule layer

Separates “looks suitable” from “can operate” by checking hard F&B infrastructure and regulatory constraints.

Financial layer

Converts assumptions into NPV, IRR, payback and downside scenarios, showing what rent or traffic must change.
